Yes. We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption for all data. Daily automated backups on secure Google Firebase servers with global redundancy. We are ISO certified and fully tax compliant. Role-based access controls let you manage exactly who sees what.

Can I import data from QuickBooks or Xero?

Absolutely! You can import all customers, items, opening balances, and past invoices from QuickBooks or Xero in under 5 minutes. Our support team will assist you for free during the migration process.

Can I access it from multiple devices?

Yes, it's 100% cloud-based. Log in from any laptop, desktop, tablet, or mobile with internet. All data syncs in real-time across all devices and all team members.
