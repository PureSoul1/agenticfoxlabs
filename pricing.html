Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a free 15-minute live demo tailored to your business. After purchase, we provide a 7-day money-back guarantee — full refund, no questions asked.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The price you see is the price you pay. All plans include unlimited users, cloud backup, and free updates. Optional add-ons like custom domains are clearly listed.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference. When downgrading, the credit applies to future billing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and international payments via Stripe. For enterprise plans, we also support wire transfers and purchase orders.

Is my data safe if I cancel?

You can export all your data at any time. After cancellation, we retain your data for 30 days so you can download everything before permanent deletion.
